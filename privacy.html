隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

本工具只供擁有者本人使用。它只會存取自己在 Google 雲端硬碟中建立的檔案（drive.file 權限），用來存放加密備份；不讀取其他檔案，不收集、分享或出售任何資料給第三方。

This tool is used only by its owner. It accesses only the files it creates in the owner's Google Drive (drive.file scope) to store encrypted backups. It does not read other files and does not collect, share, or sell any data.
